Draggy

Your iPhone and Mac, one screen.

Hold your iPhone next to your Mac and drag a file off its edge. It slides onto the Mac and lands on your Desktop right where it crossed. Drag from Finder to the screen edge to send it back.

Get Draggy How it works
Free for Mac, iPhone and iPad · Local network only · No account

Try it: drag a file onto the iPhone, or select one and press Return.

Getting Started

Pair once. Then just drag.

Pair with a six-digit code the first time. After that, the two find each other on their own whenever both apps are open.

YouDraggy
Hold the iPhone next to the MacTell Draggy which side of the screen it sits on, then press and hold Connect.The Mac shows a code. Type it on the phone and they are paired.
Drag a file off the edgeLong-press a file and drag it to the edge facing the Mac.A glow runs around the phone and the file appears on the Mac's screen.
Let goRelease it over the edge.It lands on your Desktop where it crossed, or in a folder you choose.
Send one backDrop a file from Finder on the Mac's screen edge.It shows up in your iPhone's grid.
Inside Draggy

Made to feel like one device.

Files move over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted, straight from one device to the other.

Both directions

iPhone to Mac by dragging off the edge, Mac to iPhone by dropping on the screen edge.

Draggy Info

Nothing leaves your devices.

Draggy talks only to your own paired devices, on your local network. There is no server to send anything to.

Account
None. Nothing to sign up for.
Cloud
None. No analytics, nothing tracked.
Network
Your local network only. Never mobile data, never the internet.
Encryption
Between paired devices, with a key each pair agrees through a six-digit code.
Clipboard
Read only when you ask. Never in the background.
Crash reports
Stay on your device unless you turn them on and export them yourself.
Price
Free.
